The Legend of Chhatrapati

The Visionary King

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was a visionary leader who founded the Maratha Empire in the 17th century. His innovative military tactics, administrative reforms, and commitment to justice made him a revered figure in Indian history.

Chapters of Swarajya

Shivaji Maharaj established a council of eight ministers to ensure efficient governance. This included roles like the Peshwa (Prime Minister) and Amatya (Finance Minister).

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj is recognized as the "Father of the Indian Navy" for his visionary 17th-century maritime strategy,
which was integral to his conception of Hindavi Swarajya (self-rule).His approach combined naval intelligence, local shipbuilding, and a robust defense structure to secure the
Konkan coastline against European colonial powers and regional rivals like the Siddis. he built a formidable force and sea forts like Sindhudurg to protect the coast from colonial powers.

Shivaji Maharaj was crowned at Raigad Fort in 1674. Raigad became the capital of the Maratha Empire and symbolizes the birth of Hindavi Swaraj.
The fort, located in Maharashtra's Sahyadri range, is renowned for its strategic and historical importance.
