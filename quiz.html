Empire Administration

The Council of Eight

Shivaji Maharaj established a council of eight ministers, known as the Ashta Pradhan, to ensure smooth governance. Each minister was responsible for a specific department like Finance, Internal Security, and Foreign Affairs.

Ryotwari System

He abolished the oppressive Zamindari system and introduced the Ryotwari system, where the state dealt directly with the farmers. This ensured that peasants were not exploited by middle-men.

Father of Indian Navy

Maharaj realized that "He who has the navy, has the sea." He built a formidable naval force and sea forts like Sindhudurg and Vijaydurg to protect the Konkan coast.
